RAW Catalogue

Vintage Vocoder for MPC

A free stereo vocoder for standalone Akai MPCs. Play a voice or sample from the pads or the sequencer, with eight carrier oscillators, a multimode filter and left/right split routing.

Download and install

Install on your MPC (recommended)

If you’re setting up the loader for the first time, use RAW MPC USB Creator on your Mac or Windows PC to prepare a USB stick. Download USB Creator · Set up the loader for your MPC OS version

Connect your MPC to the internet for the install. Open RAW MPC Plugin Loader, choose RAW Catalogue and install Vintage Vocoder FX. To play the vocoder with MIDI on MPC OS 3.10.1 or later, also install Vintage Vocoder Sender. You can use both plugins offline once they’re installed.

Play the vocoder with MIDI

  1. Put a recording of your voice or another sample on an audio or Drum track. Add Vintage Vocoder FX in an insert slot on that track so the sound passes through the effect.
  2. Create a separate Plugin track and select Vintage Vocoder Sender from External Plugins.
  3. Open the FX’s Vocoder tab and set the carrier to MIDI.
  4. Play the voice or sample and hold a note on the Sender track’s pads or a connected MIDI keyboard. You can record or enter notes on the Sender track and play the sequence back with the audio.

Sender is silent. Its notes set the pitch of the FX’s internal carrier while the audio shapes the sound. Every Vintage Vocoder FX instance set to MIDI follows the same Sender notes. Insert the FX on additional audio tracks to play them together.

Carrier modes

Carrier is a routing choice on the Vocoder tab. After recalling a preset or saved project, check that Carrier is set to MIDI if you want it to follow Sender.

  • Internal gives you a fixed pitch carrier without a Sender track. The oscillator pitch controls tune the carrier.
  • MIDI takes its pitch from incoming notes. Use Transpose to shift the played pitches and the oscillator level, tune and waveform controls to shape the carrier.
  • Left mod and Right mod take the modulator from the named stereo input channel and the carrier from the other channel.
  • Carrier test lets you hear the fixed carrier directly and check the oscillator settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Sender is missing. Look in the instrument browser on a Plugin track. FX appears in the insert browser. Check that both Enable Enhancements and External Plugin Support are on, then save your project and restart MPC after a manual installation.
  • The FX is silent in MIDI mode. Play a longer note on the Sender track while the audio plays. Check that MIDI note-ons advances in Sender and the FX. If it advances in both, check that audio is reaching the insert and at least one carrier oscillator is enabled.
  • Only Sender counts notes. Check that FX is set to MIDI and both plugins are from the same release. Open the FX from External Plugins if the project still uses an older Synths copy.
  • You hear only the original audio. Check that the Vocoder is On and start with Dry mix at 0%.
