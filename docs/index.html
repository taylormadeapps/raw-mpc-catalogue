Choose your MPC OS version

On your MPC, go to Menu → Preferences → Info to check your version.

  • MPC OS 3.9.1 and earlier

    Gen 1 MPCs. Install RAW from the USB prompt.

  • MPC OS 3.10.1 and later

    Gen 1 and Gen 2 MPCs. Enable External Plugin Support, then open RAW under External Plugins.

MPC OS 3.10.0 is unsupported.
